# LifePath – Game Design

**Pitch:** Live one person's whole life in a fictionalised modern Norway – from the
maternity ward to the obituary – then carry the family forward as your child.

## Pillars
1. **Every year is a decision.** Aging one year at a time is the core loop. Each year
   brings 1–3 events tailored to your situation; important decisions cannot be skipped.
2. **Consequences echo.** Choices schedule follow-ups and delayed consequences
   (a borrowed loan repaid a year later, an affair discovered two years on, an untreated
   lifestyle that becomes heart disease four years later).
3. **Circumstance shapes opportunity.** Family background, traits, health, education,
   reputation and money gate jobs, admissions, mortgages and events.
4. **No obviously right answer.** Most events trade money vs. relationships vs.
   health vs. reputation, with probabilistic outcomes biased by stats.
5. **Generations.** Death is a summary and a new beginning: inherit, continue as a child,
   and build a family legacy across many generations.

## Core loop
Age up → read new events → decide → manage career/relationships/finances/activities →
age up. The big orange age button is always one tap away and is disabled while an event
is unresolved.

## Life stages (mechanically distinct)
| Stage | Ages | What's different |
|---|---|---|
| Early childhood | 0–5 | Things happen *to* you; kindergarten, siblings, illnesses; few activities |
| Childhood | 6–12 | Automatic primary school, grades, hobbies, bullying, pocket money |
| Teen | 13–17 | Lower secondary, choice of upper secondary (general vs. vocational), first love, parties, part-time jobs, peer pressure, petty crime, driving practice |
| Young adult | 18–29 | Conscription, moving out, licence, higher education with student loans, first jobs, serious relationships, first home |
| Adult | 30–64 | Career ladders, marriage, children, property, businesses, investments, health warnings |
| Senior | 65+ | Retirement & pension, grandchildren, health decline, wills, legacy |

## Systems
- **Stats (0–100):** happiness, health, intelligence, appearance, fitness, stress,
  reputation, discipline. Health, fitness and stress drift toward targets set by age and
  lifestyle; activities and events push them around.
- **Traits:** 14 personality traits with opposites, stat bonuses, yearly drift and
  effects on events, compatibility and outcomes.
- **Education:** Norwegian system – barneskole, ungdomsskole, studieforberedende or
  yrkesfag (with paid apprenticeship), bachelor, integrated master (medicine, law,
  psychology…), master, PhD (paid). Grades on the 1–6 scale, effort, failing years,
  scholarships, Lånekassen loans with 40 % grant conversion, dropping out.
- **Careers:** 78 occupations in 16 categories with education/field/stat/experience
  requirements, salary ranges, stress, prestige and 1–4 step ladders. Apply (limited per
  year) → interview chance → offer → negotiate/accept/decline. Performance, raises,
  promotions, firing, recession layoffs, unemployment benefits, retirement with pension.
- **Relationships:** parents, siblings, grandparents, friends, best friends, partners,
  spouses, exes, children, grandchildren, coworkers, bosses and enemies. Closeness, trust,
  attraction, loyalty; personality compatibility; neglect makes friendships fade;
  19 interactions including gifts, vacations, proposing, trying for a baby, adoption,
  borrowing/collecting money, divorce and reconciliation.
- **NPCs:** age with the world, get jobs, marry, have children (your grandchildren),
  retire and die (with grief and inheritance).
- **Economy:** boom/normal/recession cycle, inflation-driven price index, policy rate,
  stock and housing markets. Simplified Norwegian tax (trygdeavgift, 22 % flat tax,
  trinnskatt, wealth tax), living costs by lifestyle, rent by city size, child costs and
  benefits, student loans, mortgages (15 % equity, 5× income), consumer loans,
  bankruptcy (gjeldsordning), pension accrual.
- **Assets:** apartments, houses, cabins, rental units, farms, commercial property, cars,
  motorcycles, boats, luxury goods; value, condition, maintenance, insurance,
  depreciation/appreciation, rental income, linked mortgages, renovation.
- **Businesses:** 7 business types with capital requirements, volatility, growth levels.
- **Activities:** 16 categories, ~80 activities with costs, risks, yearly limits,
  cooldowns and diminishing returns.
- **Health:** 20 conditions (acute, chronic, mental, serious), 4 addictions, public vs.
  private treatment, rehab, Gompertz mortality modified by health, genetics, illness and
  difficulty.
- **Crime & justice:** abstract crimes with detection chance; arrest → trial (plead,
  defend yourself, hire a lawyer) → fine or prison; records block clean-record jobs and
  hurt hiring; prison life events and activities; release and rehabilitation.
- **Legacy:** life summary, generated obituary, legacy score, family tree, continue as a
  child with inheritance, unlimited generations in one save.
- **Achievements:** 44 permanent achievements (some hidden) that unlock starting scenarios
  (Heir, Prodigy, Born Star, Dynasty).

## Tone
Warm, a little wry, distinctly Norwegian (17. mai, dugnad, russetid, hyttetur, Lånekassen,
NAV), never mean-spirited. Crime and tragedy are handled narratively and abstractly.

## Monetisation
None. No real-money gambling, no pay-to-win. All gambling uses in-game money.
